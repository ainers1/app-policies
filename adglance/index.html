Ad Earnings Glance

Connect your own Google AdMob account and see estimated earnings, impressions, eCPM, clicks, CTR, ad requests and match rate for today, yesterday, the last 7 or 28 days, this month and last month — broken down by app, country, ad format and ad unit. The UI language can be changed in Settings (Korean, English, Japanese, Simplified Chinese, Spanish).

The app requests a single read-only AdMob API scope (admob.report), has no developer server, and offers a sample-data mode that works without an AdMob account. Privacy policy · Disconnect & data deletion
